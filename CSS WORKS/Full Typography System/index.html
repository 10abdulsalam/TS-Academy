Typography system

Creating a readable rhythm for modern editorial design

Building hierarchy without sacrificing comfort

Good typography is less about decoration and more about control. A strong type system gives each block of information a clear role, helping the eye move naturally from headline to supporting detail and finally into the body copy itself.

When scale, spacing, and line length are tuned carefully, reading becomes effortless. Long passages feel engaging instead of dense, and every visual cue works together to support the writer's message rather than compete with it.

In practice, this means choosing a distinctive serif for headings, a clean sans-serif for the text, and a measured rhythm across paragraphs. The best systems keep their structure quiet enough to be invisible while still giving the page enough personality to feel memorable.
